Breathe Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

[Developer name] (“we”) built Breathe (the “app”), a breathing practice app. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

  • The app has no sign-up, account, or server of its own.
  • Your practice history and preferences stay on your device and are never sent to us.
  • The app shows Google AdMob ads. Google may collect some information, such as the advertising ID, to serve them.
  • Your practice history is never used for ads.

1. Information stored only on your device

The app keeps the following information on your device to make its features work. We cannot receive or see it.

ItemDetailsPurpose
Practice historyProgram, duration, number of rounds, and time each session endedHistory screen and daily, weekly, and monthly statistics
PreferencesDefault program, background sound and volume, voice and vibration guidance, reminder on/off and time, custom rhythm, rounds and scene, app languageKeeping the settings you chose

Device backup is turned off for the app, so this information is not copied to cloud backups.

2. Information Google collects for ads

The app shows Google AdMob native ads on the completion screen and in the program list. To serve and measure these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK included in the app may send the following directly to Google. We do not collect or store this information ourselves.

  • Advertising ID (Android advertising ID)
  • Device information such as IP address, device model, OS version, and language
  • Ad interactions such as impressions and clicks
  • On devices that support the Android Privacy Sandbox (Topics and Attribution), interest and ad performance information through those APIs

To learn how Google uses this information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Ad consent and your choices

  • Where consent is required by law, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent to personalized ads through Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform) on first launch. No ads are requested until consent has been checked.
  • In those regions you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy settings in the app.
  • You can delete or reset your advertising ID in your Android device settings under Privacy → Ads. Once deleted, it is no longer used for personalized ads.

The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or other personal data. Voice guidance uses an offline voice installed on your device and does not send any text off the device.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not share your personal information with third parties or ask anyone to process it on our behalf. As described in section 2, Google LLC collects information directly through the SDK included in the app in order to show ads.

RecipientPurposeInformationRetention
Google LLC (United States)Serving and measuring ads, preventing invalid clicksInformation listed in section 2As set out in the Google Privacy Policy

This information may be transferred over the network to Google servers outside your country, including the United States, whenever an ad is requested. If you do not want this, you can stop using the app or opt out of personalized ads as described in section 3.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Practice history and preferences on your device are deleted immediately when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Settings → Apps → Breathe → Storage → Clear data.
  • We keep nothing on a server, so there is nothing else for us to delete.

7. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, delete, or stop the processing of your personal information. You can delete the information on your device yourself as described in section 6, and manage the ad information Google processes as described in section 3 or in My Ad Center. For anything else, contact us using the details below.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under 14 in Korea), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security

Practice history and preferences are kept in the app’s private storage on Android, which other apps cannot access. The app does not send this information to any server of ours.
