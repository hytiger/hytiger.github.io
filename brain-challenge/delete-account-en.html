Brain Challenge Account and Data Deletion

App: Brain Challenge · Developer: hytiger

Brain Challenge can be used without signing in. If you participate in the leaderboard, a randomly generated anonymous account is created and your nickname and best game scores are stored on the server. You can delete this information using the method below.

Delete in the app

  1. Open the app.
  2. Tap Settings (the gear icon) at the top right of the home screen.
  3. Tap Leaderboard > Delete leaderboard data, then tap Delete.

Your nickname, leaderboard records, and anonymous account are deleted from the server immediately.

If you have already uninstalled the app

Your leaderboard account is anonymous and can be accessed only on that device. Once the app is uninstalled, the operator cannot tell which account belongs to you and cannot delete it upon a separate request.

Instead, leaderboard accounts inactive for 90 days are automatically deleted. Your nickname and leaderboard records will be deleted 90 days after you uninstall the app.

Information deleted

  • Nickname, best scores by game and difficulty (all-time and weekly), and anonymous account
  • Synchronization usage and ad viewing verification records

Information retained

  • No account data remains on the server. The server provider’s operational logs (such as IP addresses) are automatically deleted after its retention period.
  • Game records and settings stored on your device are separate from server data. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Automatic deletion

Even without a request, leaderboard accounts inactive for 90 days are automatically deleted. Afterward, another user can use the same nickname. Accounts without a nickname are deleted 30 days after creation.

For more information, see the Privacy Policy.
