Brain Challenge Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

Summary

  • We do not collect personal information such as your name, phone number, email address, or location.
  • Only when you participate in the leaderboard, we send a randomly generated account ID, your chosen nickname, and your best game scores to the server. If you do not participate, none of this information is sent.
  • For advertising (Google AdMob), Google processes your advertising ID and other information.
  • Game records and settings are stored only on your device and cannot be viewed by the operator.
  • You can immediately delete your server data at any time through Settings > Leaderboard > Delete leaderboard data in the app.

hytiger (the “operator”) complies with applicable laws, including Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act, and processes the personal information of Brain Challenge (the “app”) users as described below.

1. Personal information processed

WhenInformation
When participating in the leaderboard (optional)Randomly generated anonymous account ID, nickname, best scores by game and difficulty and the time they were achieved, app version, synchronization usage records (date and whether free or ad-supported), and last access date
When synchronizing after watching an adAd viewing verification records (ad transaction ID and account ID)
When ads are displayed in the appGoogle AdMob processes the advertising ID, device and app information, IP address, ad usage records, and similar information (see section 5)
When connecting to the serverThe IP address and connection time may be included in server operational logs

Game result records, best scores, play counts, and sound, vibration, and theme settings are stored only on your device and are not sent to the server.

2. Purposes of processing

  • Providing all-time and weekly leaderboards and displaying nicknames
  • Managing daily synchronization limits (free and ad-supported)
  • Preventing misuse, including impossible scores
  • Verifying ad views and providing ads

3. Retention and deletion

InformationRetention period
Nickname, leaderboard records, and account IDUntil deleted by the user. However, accounts inactive for 90 days are automatically deleted (accounts without a nickname are deleted 30 days after creation).
Synchronization usage and ad viewing verification recordsAutomatically deleted after 7 days
Server operational logs (such as IP addresses)Automatically deleted according to the server provider’s (Supabase’s) retention period

When the retention period ends or a user requests deletion, the data is promptly deleted from the server database and cannot be recovered.

4. Information visible to other users

When you participate in the leaderboard, your nickname and scores are visible to other users on the leaderboard. Your account ID is not public. The operator does not sell or provide personal information to third parties.

5. Service providers and international transfers

The operator entrusts the following providers with processing personal information to operate the app. This involves transferring personal information outside Korea.

6. Your rights and how to exercise them

  • Delete immediately: Select Settings > Leaderboard > Delete leaderboard data in the app to immediately delete your nickname, leaderboard records, and account from the server.
  • Change your nickname: Select Settings > Leaderboard > Nickname (once every 7 days).
  • If you have already uninstalled the app: The leaderboard account is anonymous and can only be accessed on that device, so the operator cannot identify and delete an individual account. It will instead be automatically deleted after 90 days of inactivity. See the account and data deletion guide for details.
  • For other inquiries, contact the email address in section 9.

Uninstalling the app also deletes locally stored game records and leaderboard account information, so you cannot access the old leaderboard account again.

7. Advertising ID and personalized ads

  • On Android, you can delete or reset your advertising ID under Settings > Privacy (or Google) > Ads.
  • In the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and other applicable regions, the app asks for advertising consent when first launched.
  • Rewarded ads are shown only when you choose to watch one to increase your synchronization allowance.

8. Children under 14

The app’s leaderboard is intended for users aged 14 and over. Children under 14 should not participate. If we learn that information about a child under 14 has been collected, we will promptly delete it.

9. Privacy officer and contact

Privacy officer: hytiger
Email: hypositive@gmail.com

You may also contact Korea’s Personal Information Infringement Report Center (privacy.kisa.or.kr, 118 within Korea) or Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (www.kopico.go.kr, 1833-6972) for assistance with privacy concerns.

10. Security measures

  • All communication between the app and server is encrypted using HTTPS.
  • The app cannot directly access the server database; it exchanges necessary information only through designated functions.
  • Server administration keys are kept on the server and are not included in the app.

11. Changes to this policy

Changes to this policy will be announced on this page and in the app’s update notes at least 7 days before they take effect. Material changes to users’ rights will be announced 30 days in advance.
